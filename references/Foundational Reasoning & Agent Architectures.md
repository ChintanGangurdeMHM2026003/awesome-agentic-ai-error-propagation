### Chain-of-Thought Prompting

- **Paper:** *Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models*
- **Authors:** Jason Wei et al.
- **Year:** 2022
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2022
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/068431-1800)
- **Why it matters:** Establishes the importance of intermediate reasoning steps for complex tasks and provides a foundation for understanding multi-step reasoning trajectories.

### ReAct

- **Paper:** *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models*
- **Authors:** Shunyu Yao et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** ICLR 2023
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.03629)
- **Why it matters:** Combines reasoning with actions and environmental observations, making it highly relevant to multi-step agent trajectories and error propagation.

### Toolformer

- **Paper:** *Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools*
- **Authors:** Timo Schick et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-2997)
- **Why it matters:** Studies how language models select, invoke, and incorporate external tools, highlighting potential sources of errors between tool calls.

### Tree of Thoughts

- **Paper:** *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models*
- **Authors:** Shunyu Yao et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/271db9922b8d1f4dd7aaef84ed5ac703-Abstract.html)
- **Why it matters:** Explores multiple reasoning paths, evaluation, and backtracking, providing insight into how early reasoning choices can affect later outcomes.

### HuggingGPT

- **Paper:** *HuggingGPT: Solving AI Tasks with ChatGPT and its Friends in Hugging Face*
- **Authors:** Yongliang Shen et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-1657)
- **Why it matters:** Uses an LLM to plan tasks, select models, execute subtasks, and aggregate results, creating a multi-step pipeline where intermediate errors can propagate.
