### Reflexion

- **Paper:** *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning*
- **Authors:** Noah Shinn et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/1b44b878bb782e6954cd888628510e90-Abstract-Conference.html)
- **Why it matters:** Enables agents to learn from previous failures using verbal feedback and episodic memory, providing a mechanism for recovery from mistakes.

### Self-Refine

- **Paper:** *Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback*
- **Authors:** Aman Madaan et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-2019)
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates iterative generation, self-feedback, and refinement as a way to detect and correct errors before they persist.

### CRITIC

- **Paper:** *CRITIC: Large Language Models Can Self-Correct with Tool-Interactive Critiquing*
- **Authors:** Zhibin Gou et al.
- **Year:** 2024
- **Venue:** ICLR 2024
- **Link:** [Paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/fef126561bbf9d4467dbb8d27334b8fe-Abstract-Conference.html)
- **Why it matters:** Uses external tools to verify model outputs and provide corrective feedback, demonstrating the value of independent validation in agent systems.

### Where LLM Agents Fail and How They Can Learn From Failures

- **Paper:** *Where LLM Agents Fail and How They Can Learn From Failures*
- **Authors:** Kunlun Zhu et al.
- **Year:** 2025
- **Venue:** arXiv preprint
- **Link:** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25370)
- **Why it matters:** Directly investigates cascading failures in LLM agents and introduces AgentErrorTaxonomy, AgentErrorBench, and AgentDebug for failure classification, diagnosis, and recovery.
