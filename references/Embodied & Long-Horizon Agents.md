### SayCan

- **Paper:** *Do As I Can, Not As I Say: Grounding Language in Robotic Affordances*
- **Authors:** Brian Ichter et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), PMLR 205
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/ichter23a.html)
- **Why it matters:** Connects language-model planning with executable robotic skills and demonstrates how errors can affect long sequences of physical actions.

### Inner Monologue

- **Paper:** *Inner Monologue: Embodied Reasoning through Planning with Language Models*
- **Authors:** Wenlong Huang et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), PMLR 205
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/huang23c.html)
- **Why it matters:** Incorporates environmental feedback into language-model planning, allowing agents to adapt their behavior during long-horizon tasks.
