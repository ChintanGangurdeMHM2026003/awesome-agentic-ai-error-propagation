### AutoGen

- **Paper:** *AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation*
- **Authors:** Qingyun Wu et al.
- **Year:** 2024
- **Venue:** COLM 2024
- **Link:** [Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BAakY1h3Zf)
- **Why it matters:** Provides a framework for multiple agents to communicate and collaborate, creating opportunities for information and errors to propagate between agents.

### CAMEL

- **Paper:** *CAMEL: Communicative Agents for "Mind" Exploration of Large Language Model Society*
- **Authors:** Guohao Li et al.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Link:** [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-2264)
- **Why it matters:** Studies autonomous cooperation between role-playing agents and provides a setting for analyzing communication-driven propagation of information and mistakes.
